HEXBOUND - STAGE 4 - TESTER

Goal of this stage: one full regression pass and a second playtest, so the feature freeze happens on a build that works. Dates are in the team plan, section "Timeline".


1. Full regression pass

Run every test case and every automated test on main, not only the ones for new work. Play a full run in each supported browser, or split the browsers with a teammate.


2. Second playtest

At least 2 people outside the team who did not play in stage 3. Same table as before. Check that the stage 3 balance changes hold, and that the polish didn't break anything they touch.


3. Check the texts

Go through the PM's final texts in the game: card text matches what the card does, no text is cut off or overflows.


Hand off at the end of stage 4

- To each owner: bug reports, marked "must fix before release" or "can wait".
- To the PM: the second playtest results, and whether the build is ready for the freeze.


Stage 4 is done for you when the regression pass and the second playtest are written up and every "must fix" bug has an owner.

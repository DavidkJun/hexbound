HEXBOUND - STAGE 5 - TESTER

Goal of this stage: confirm that the release build delivers everything the team committed to. Dates are in the team plan, section "Timeline".


1. Acceptance test

Go through the must-have list in the design document, section 5, item by item, on the deployed release build. For each item write: passed or failed, and the evidence (a test, a screenshot, a playtest note).


2. Final numbers for the report

Collect for the PM: number of test cases and automated tests, bugs found and fixed, playtest results against the targets (from stages 3 and 4).


3. Check last fixes

Every fix merged in this stage gets checked again on the deployed build.


Hand off at the end of stage 5

- To the PM: the acceptance table and the final numbers, for the report and the presentation.
- To the programmer: any must-have item that failed.


Stage 5 is done for you when the acceptance table is complete and every must-have item has passed, or the PM has decided what to do about it.

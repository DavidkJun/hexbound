HEXBOUND - STAGE 2 - UI / LEVEL DESIGNER

Goal of this stage: maps 2 and 3 are in the game, and every screen of a full run exists. Dates are in the team plan, section "Timeline".


1. Maps 2 and 3

Build data/levels/frostpeak.json and data/levels/ashen.json from the PM's route intent, and add them to levels.json.
- Frostpeak: ice is slow and chills the next fight. Make the ice-free route clearly longer.
- Ashen Citadel: lava deals damage and can kill. Every lava tile must be avoidable by a longer route.
- Before code depends on them, send both maps to the tester to check the food math against startFood.


2. The stage 2 screens

From design/screens.md, stage 2: chest, locked chest, village, shrine, mystery event, event result, run over and score, pause menu.
- The village is the most complex one: five actions, prices, gold left, the bounty and the deck view in removal mode. Do it first.
- Keep the others simple: a short spec line in design/screens.md, the tokens and the existing components.
- Every screen must handle the states in design/screens.md, "States every screen must handle" (can't afford, would kill you, no key and so on).


3. Hazard and modifier visuals

The map must show ice and lava clearly, and the fight must show Chilled and Rested in its modifier row. The player should see where a modifier came from.


Hand off at the end of stage 2

- To the programmer: anything the screens need that the game state does not give.
- To the tester: maps 2 and 3 for the food check, then the screens.
- To the PM: map problems that need a content or rule change.


Stage 2 is done for you when maps 2 and 3 and all stage 2 screens are merged, and a full run can be played on the deployed build.

HEXBOUND - STAGE 4 - PM / GAME DESIGNER

Goal of this stage: the game feels finished, and nothing new goes in after it. Dates are in the team plan, section "Timeline".


1. First: pick the nice-to-haves

From the design document: extra chest types, card upgrades, sound and music, a consistent art set, saving a run between sessions.
- With the time left, pick at most one or two, and only ones the owner says fit in the stage. A consistent art set is the UI / level designer's main stage 4 task anyway.
- Tell the team on the first day, so nobody starts something that gets cut.
- Everything else stays on the nice-to-have list.


2. Final text pass

Read every text the player sees: cards, enemies, signatures, blessings, events, level texts, hints, buttons (data/ and data/text.json). Check that card text matches what the card does, the spelling, and that the same thing is called the same everywhere.


3. Feature freeze

At the end of the stage, announce the feature freeze. From then on only bug fixes are merged.


Hand off at the end of stage 4

- To everyone: the chosen nice-to-haves (first day), and the feature freeze (last day).
- To the tester: the final texts, for the regression pass.


Stage 4 is done at the feature freeze.

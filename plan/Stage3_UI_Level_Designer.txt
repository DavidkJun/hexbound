HEXBOUND - STAGE 3 - UI / LEVEL DESIGNER

Goal of this stage: fix what playtests show players can't read or find, and change maps when the PM asks. Dates are in the team plan, section "Timeline".


1. Watch at least one playtest

Sit next to a player, or watch a recording. Note where they look for something and don't find it, misread a number, or click the wrong thing. That is your task list for this stage.


2. Fix readability and layout

Typical problems to look for:
- The planned route or its food cost is missed.
- The enemy intent is misread.
- The player does not see why something is unavailable (gold, energy, key).
- Map tiles of one type look too much alike.
Fix them through the tokens and the specs, as before.


3. Map changes

When the PM asks for a map change (a slot moved, a route made longer), make it and tell the tester, so the food check is repeated.


Hand off at the end of stage 3

- To the tester: each fix, for the next playtest.
- To the PM: problems that are about rules, not looks.


Stage 3 is done for you when the readability problems from the playtests are fixed and merged.

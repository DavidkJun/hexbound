| Land | Ground | Hazard | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|

**Expected:** "the game is mostly read: the map must be readable at a glance" (`Stage0_UI_Level_Designer.txt`), and slow tiles drive the route decision (pillar 1). **Actual:** see the table.

**Suggested fix:** darken the Frostpeak ice (e.g. #3F7AA6 is 3.12:1 against the ground), or add a pattern or texture to hazard tiles. Re-check the contrast with this test.
